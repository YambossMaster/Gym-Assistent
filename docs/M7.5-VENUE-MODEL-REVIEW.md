# M7.5 venue model review — 2026-09-25

> Product Owner correction clarified on 2026-09-25. This document records the
> required workflow and the mismatch with the local implementation. Reconcile it into
> [M7.5-MONTHLY-FINANCE-CONTRACT.md](M7.5-MONTHLY-FINANCE-CONTRACT.md)
> before changing schema, API, or scheduling behavior.

## The three linked workflows

1. **Student purchase:** A purchase grants lessons. It can be made before a
   Venue is chosen (**無固定場地**) or for a named Venue already known to the Coach.
   The purchase's Venue choice is an entitlement rule, not merely a Student
   profile preference or a field copied into the next scheduling draft. Each
   Venue-bound purchase contributes lessons to that Student's balance at that
   Venue. A purchase without a fixed Venue contributes lessons usable at any
   Venue.
2. **Scheduling:** Calendar one-off Sessions and fixed recurring Series use the
   same eligibility rule. A purchase without a fixed Venue can be scheduled at
   any Venue; each completed Session incurs that Venue's applicable per-Session
   cost. Venue-bound purchases can be scheduled only at Venues for which that
   Student has purchased lessons. The eligible Venue is preselected; if several
   are eligible, the Coach chooses among them in the Venue selector. A Series
   and its generated Sessions must remain consistent with that choice. Each
   **completed** Session deducts exactly one Student lesson from its applicable
   purchase balance. At a prepaid-lesson Venue, that same completion also
   deducts exactly one Venue lesson; scheduling alone deducts neither.
3. **Venue management:** The selected expense type reveals only its relevant
   fields and records. A prepaid-lesson Venue has a visible action to record
   each Venue lesson purchase, a remaining-lesson balance, and a Today notice
   when that balance is at most one. A dual-rate commission Venue treats its
   Students as Venue-supplied by default and lets the Coach mark particular
   Student–Venue relationships as Coach-supplied. A separate monthly base-salary
   toggle, amount, and monthly pay day are needed for a Venue that pays the
   Coach a salary. Each pay date adds one base-salary income row to **收支明細**
   in **本月收支**.

## Expense modes and events to reconcile

| Venue setting                             | Venue-specific controls                                  | Event described by the Product Owner                                                                                                                                               |
| ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| No expense tracking / explicitly free     | Name and selected mode                                   | No inferred cost / known zero respectively                                                                                                                                         |
| Fixed commission                          | Rate                                                     | Venue-bound Student purchase: calculate commission on that purchase once when purchased. No-fixed-Venue purchase: calculate the selected Venue's commission per completed Session. |
| Commission by customer source             | Two rates, Coach-supplied Student list                   | Venue-supplied by default; use the other rate for listed Students                                                                                                                  |
| Per-Session Venue fee                     | Amount per Session                                       | One expense per completed Session at that Venue                                                                                                                                    |
| Prepaid Venue lessons                     | Add/correct Venue lesson purchases and remaining balance | Purchase is one expense; completion consumes one Venue lesson, without charging it again                                                                                           |
| Monthly base salary (independent setting) | Enable switch, amount, and pay day (day of month)        | One income row on each configured monthly pay date in **收支明細**.                                                                                                                |

Changing a Venue rule naturally concerns Sessions and purchases associated
with that Venue. The ordinary fee editor should not make the Coach choose an
"impact scope." Existing records and dated changes may still need a concise
read-only explanation of which recorded amounts would change; any historical
correction must be explicit and must preserve completed facts until confirmed.

## Current local implementation gaps

- `Student.defaultVenueId` suggests a Venue for new scheduling. A purchase has
  an optional `venueId`, but the purchase input schema does not accept or update
  it; there is no Venue-specific Student lesson balance or eligibility check in
  Calendar, fixed Series, and Session mutation paths.
- Commission is currently derived per completed Session from its allocated
  Student purchase. For dual rates, `customerSource` is stored on each Session
  or Series and is required by the current database trigger. This conflicts
  with the proposed Student–Venue default and Coach-supplied exception list.
- The Venue editor currently opens a second **確認影響範圍** step for rule changes.
  The Product Owner does not want this as a normal choice or setup operation.
- **登錄預購** already exists in the Venue list/detail UI, but appears only after
  the current rule is prepaid. The type-dependent flow must make adding a Venue
  purchase discoverable and keep its historical records visible.
- There is no monthly base-salary setting or income event.
- Current finance calculations assume purchase income and completed-Session
  commission/rent expenses. They omit one-time commission on a Venue-bound
  purchase and monthly salary income. Finance totals cannot be treated as
  authoritative under the corrected model yet.

## Direct consequences for the revised contract

1. A completed Session uses one eligible Student lesson at its selected Venue.
   For a Venue-bound purchase, use that Venue's purchased lessons; a general
   purchase can cover a Session at any Venue. Allocate a Venue-bound lesson
   before a general lesson at the same Venue, then oldest purchase first. Do
   not silently move completed Sessions or rewrite their selected Venue.
2. For a Venue-bound purchase under a commission rule, calculate its full
   purchase commission once on the purchase date. A later completed Session
   consuming that purchase does not create a second commission expense. A
   general purchase has no Venue at purchase time, so apply the selected
   Venue's commission to each completed Session's allocated lesson price.
3. Base salary is separate from Venue expense type. Record the amount,
   currency, and monthly pay day; generate one income detail row on that date.
   Monthly totals and difference include the row in its pay month. Months
   without an enabled salary or pay date have no inferred salary row.
4. Ordinary Venue-rule changes apply according to the Venue and effective
   date; the system derives the affected records. Historical recalculation is
   a distinct correction, with a read-only consequence summary if it changes
   recorded amounts, never an editable impact-scope choice.

## Acceptance scenarios for the revised contract

- Buy lessons with **無固定場地**, then schedule and complete Sessions at two
  Venues with different expense modes. Verify the Student lesson balance and
  each Venue expense without charging a prepaid purchase twice.
- Buy lessons for Venue A and Venue B. Calendar and fixed Series show only
  eligible Venues, preselect the sole eligible Venue, and offer a selector for
  both. Try changing to Venue C and verify the server rejects an ineligible
  Session or Series as well as a stale edit.
- Use a dual-rate Venue with an ordinary Venue-supplied Student and a marked
  Coach-supplied Student. Verify both rates without asking for source on every
  Session.
- Add Venue lesson purchases until its balance moves from two to one, then
  back above one, and then to one again. Verify the Today notice lifecycle.
- Enable Venue base salary with an amount and pay day. Verify one income row
  on each pay date, its inclusion in that month's totals, and no row in an
  earlier month.
