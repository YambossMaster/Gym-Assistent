# M8 Taiwan code-gated Beta: privacy and terms research

Research snapshot: 2026-10-01. This is a source-backed drafting aid, not a legal opinion or an approved M8 Contract. The Product Owner later removed paid Taiwan-counsel review as an M8 release gate and chose a lean Local + Production MVP. Recheck the effective law and actual production providers/configuration before publishing terms or inviting real Coaches.

## Scope and legally material facts

Gym Assistant plans a Taiwan Web/PWA, free code-gated Beta. An authenticated Coach owns a private Workspace; a Student has no account. The current schema stores Student name, phone, age range and private notes, along with scheduling, lesson, training and finance records. A Training Result or reschedule capability link can expose a narrow public view. The existing product boundary excludes medical/health records and body weight. These are project facts, not a conclusion that free service or Coach-entered Student records fall outside privacy law. [ROADMAP](ROADMAP.md), [Student migration](../supabase/migrations/20260907144520_initial_identity_workspace_student.sql), [training migration](../supabase/migrations/20260915090210_m5_training.sql), [capability-link migration](../supabase/migrations/20260915111114_m6_public_capability_links.sql).

Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) includes directly or indirectly identifying data, defines the operator (including a natural person) as a non-government agency, and treats storage, retrieval, deletion and internal transmission as processing. Coach account data and identifiable Student records therefore require a stated purpose, a lawful collection basis, purpose-limited use and security measures; a free Beta is not a general exemption. [PDPA arts. 2, 5, 19–20, 27](https://law.pdpc.gov.tw/LawContentHistory.aspx?hid=1&id=FL010627).

**Version warning:** the official consolidated page includes amendments promulgated 2025-11-11 that are marked wholly or partly **not yet effective**. This note uses the official previous-version text for changed arts. 12 and 27. In particular, do not present the amended art. 12 authority-reporting rule or replacement art. 20-1 as already in force without checking commencement. [Official current page and warning](https://law.pdpc.gov.tw/LawContent.aspx?id=FL010627), [official previous version](https://law.pdpc.gov.tw/LawContentHistory.aspx?hid=1&id=FL010627).

**2026-10-01 Product Owner revision:** the proposed Free Beta has no scheduled database backup and
may permanently lose Coach and Student records. Supabase recommends offsite exports for Free, but
that recommendation is not a statement that every service is legally required to implement that
exact method. Conversely, the Product Owner's risk acceptance or a Coach's checkbox cannot by
itself establish that no-backup operations meet the applicable duty to take proportionate measures
against data loss. Check the actual data categories, controls, disclosure and incident process
before admitting real Coaches. The current public draft must not
promise backup or restore. [Supabase Free backup guidance](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/backups),
[PDPA art. 27](https://law.pdpc.gov.tw/LawContentHistory.aspx?hid=1&id=FL010627),
[Enforcement Rules art. 12](https://law.pdpc.gov.tw/LawContent.aspx?id=FL010628).

## Decisions needed for publication

1. Identify the legal operator, public contact channel, hosting region, email sender and other actual processors. A personal Gmail address may be the public contact if the Product Owner selects it and it remains reachable; the notice needs a stable rights/contact route.
2. Choose a retention and exit rule for account, Student, feedback, telemetry and logs. Record explicitly that the current Free Beta has no scheduled database backup; if backups are added later, specify their retention and deletion. “Retain forever” or “never delete” is incompatible with an unconditional claim that all personal data can remain after purpose expiry; assess art. 11 exceptions case by case.
3. Decide whether Coach-entered Student notices/requests are fulfilled by the Coach, the service operator, or jointly under a documented workflow, including how to avoid exposing another person's records. The account-free Student and public-link flows make this a product as well as legal decision.
4. Confirm whether any notes or feedback can contain medical/health-check data despite the intended exclusion. [PDPA art. 6](https://law.pdpc.gov.tw/LawContentHistory.aspx?hid=1&id=FL010627) imposes stricter conditions for enumerated sensitive categories; prohibit unnecessary entry and review actual UX/copy.
5. Before publication, make the final notice and terms match the actual Coach/Student roles, contact, retention, providers and effective law. Paid counsel review is optional under the Product Owner's revised M8 gate; this does not remove statutory obligations or make a generic template sufficient without factual adaptation.
